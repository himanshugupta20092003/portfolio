The One He Posted Himself

Personal reels and content·Written, shot, edited and posted by me

What I did

Everything else on this site was made for someone else's brand. This is the one where I'm the client, which is the hardest brief there is: no approvals to hide behind, and nobody to blame for the hook.

I write the scripts, shoot them, cut them, and post them, then read what the numbers say and change the next one. It's the fastest feedback loop I have on content, a reel tells you within an hour whether the first three seconds earned the rest.

Not all of it is a case study. Some of it is just me having a good time on camera, and a personal experience worth keeping. That's the point of the account, and it's also why the tone here is looser than anything I'd hand a client.

What follows is the work as it actually ran, embedded straight from Instagram rather than screenshotted.
