Episode 01 · Growth Systems

The One in the Founder's Office

Mavlers·Jan 2026 – Present·Founder's Office Associate

What I did

Took ownership of the entire HubSpot instance and started with the unglamorous part: cleaning it. Data integrity and data sanity first, because every report built on a messy CRM is a confident wrong answer.

From there I restructured the internal processes and rebuilt the workflows around them, automating the stages that were being held together by someone remembering to do them. Implemented lead scoring so the pipeline sorts itself by intent, and made sure no lead quietly falls out of the funnel between stages.

Went back to the top of the funnel and did the thinking that should come before any campaign: building out buyer personas, defining the ideal customer profile, and analysing new markets worth entering rather than the ones we happened to already be in.

Ran and iterated on both inbound and outbound campaigns against those personas, treating them as two genuinely different problems rather than one funnel with two doors. Worked on conversion rate optimisation across the journey, because the cheapest growth available is usually the traffic you're already paying for.

Rebuilt reporting on top of a clean instance, which finally gave marketing and sales the same version of the truth: the handoff between them is now explicit rather than assumed. Built multi-channel integrations so the data lands in one place instead of four, which is what makes the whole process measurable in the first place.

Sitting next to the founder means seeing which numbers actually move the business, not which ones are easy to chart.
