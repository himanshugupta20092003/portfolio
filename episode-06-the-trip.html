Episode 06 · Brand & Content

The One With the Trip

PAX Travel·Freelance, attraction and brochure creation

What I did

PAX Travel is the trips side of the same business: itineraries, local experiences and on-ground support in Mauritius. My part was design and content.

Planned and designed the trip itineraries themselves, which is a design problem before it's a travel one: a day has to read clearly on a page before anyone can picture themselves inside it. Then the brochures that carry them, plus the creative and content around each trip.

The work has to hold the specifics a traveller actually decides on: what a day looks like, what it costs, and who handles it when something goes wrong.
