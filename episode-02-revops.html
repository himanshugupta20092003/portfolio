Episode 02 · Growth Systems

The One With RevOps

One Metric (formerly Growtomation)·May 2025 – Dec 2025·RevOps Associate

What I did

Ran end-to-end delivery across multiple clients at once, which meant owning the relationship as much as the work: scoping it, communicating through it, and being the person the client called when something in their CRM stopped behaving.

Built and untangled complex workflows and full customer lifecycles inside HubSpot, and handled the problems that came with them: diagnosing what broke, mitigating the risk before it reached the client's pipeline, and fixing the root cause rather than the symptom.

Ran email campaigns and wrote the content that went into them, including blogs, so the strategy and the words weren't handed between two people who'd never spoken.

Built website automations and ran entire marketing campaigns across several accounts, then built the dashboards that showed whether any of it had worked — reporting people actually opened, instead of a deck nobody asked for twice.

This is where marketing stopped being a guess for me.
