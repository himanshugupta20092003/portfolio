The One With the Coffee House

PAS Specialty Coffee·Freelance, full brand surface

What I did

A café lives or dies on three things: whether someone can find it, whether they trust it, and whether they want to sit in it. I own all three.

Finding it is the Google Business Profile: categories, photos, posts, local discovery, plus Zomato and the other aggregators, where most people decide before they've ever seen the place. Trusting it is the reviews, which I manage and respond to, because in a beach town the last three reviews are the storefront. Wanting to sit in it is everything else.

That part started from nothing: the logo, the brand identity, and the rules that keep it consistent, then every place a customer would actually meet it. The menu on the table, the brochures, the banners, the website, and the design elements inside the room itself.

And running underneath all of it, the social and content as a standing function: strategy, monthly calendars, carousels, captions, community response, and a Google Ads plan built for local intent rather than reach.
