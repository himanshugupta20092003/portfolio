About Me

I started as an engineer, learning to build systems that work whether or not anyone's watching. Somewhere around CGPA and a placement season, I noticed the systems I actually wanted to build weren't software. They were the ones that tell a business whether it's growing.

So I moved into marketing. Most of my week goes to the work you're here for: content that sounds like a person wrote it, social that earns its place on a feed, campaigns built to be measured, and websites people actually stay on. The other half runs lifecycle marketing and the whole HubSpot ecosystem underneath it: pipelines, sales process, the complicated workflows. One half makes it, the other half proves it worked.

One joke, since you asked: I minored in judging bad design. Nobody requested it, everybody gets it.
