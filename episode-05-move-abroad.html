Episode 05 · Brand & Content

The One Where They All Move Abroad

PAX Edutainment Pvt. Ltd.·Freelance, internal creatives

What I did

Moving a 20-year-old across the world is a high-stakes decision made mostly on Instagram. Two people have to say yes: the student, who wants the adventure, and the parent, who wants the safety, and everything I make has to work on both of them at once.

The print side is the parent's half. Multi-page, print-ready brochures across their programmes, including a full rebuild of multiple brochures.

The social side is the student's. End-to-end Instagram management: content strategy, monthly calendars, reel and post scripts written for an audience that scrolls fast and trusts slowly, and the campaign collateral around it.
